About Semesta

The name Semesta comes from the Indonesian word for universe, representing the many things that make up a student's world. It reflects the idea that every student has their own universe of responsibilities, ambitions, and dreams. Semesta aims to provide a simple, organized space to manage academic tasks, schedules, and goals, helping students find balance, stay focused, and make progress at their own pace.
